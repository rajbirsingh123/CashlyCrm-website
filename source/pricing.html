FAQ

Questions teams usually ask before getting started.

Is this pricing per user?

Yes. Cashly is priced at CAD$199 per user monthly, or CAD$1,910.40 per user yearly after the 20% annual discount.

Are the AI tools included?

Yes. CallSense, DealSense, DocSense, Broker Copilot, calling tools, onboarding, training, and support are all included in the same plan.

Do drips and outreach tools come with the plan?

Yes. Email drips, SMS drips, Auto Dialer, and campaign calling tools are included, so your team can run outreach without bolting on separate systems.

Do larger teams get better pricing?

Yes. Teams over 5, 20, 50, and 100 users get additional discounts on both monthly and annual pricing. If you are planning a larger rollout, we can tailor the rollout with your team.
