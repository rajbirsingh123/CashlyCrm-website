About Cashly CRM

We are building a CRM that fits how mortgage teams actually work.

Cashly CRM exists to reduce friction inside the brokerage workflow. Our focus is simple: give teams a cleaner way to manage intake, lender matching, file movement, and follow-up without relying on scattered spreadsheets and disconnected notes.

Workflow clarity Make the next step obvious for every active file.
Team visibility Keep ownership, progress, and context visible across the brokerage.
Better service Help teams respond faster and communicate with more confidence.
Why we built it

Broker teams need a better operating system than inboxes, sticky notes, and disconnected spreadsheets.

Mortgage files move quickly and often require coordination across brokers, assistants, lenders, and borrowers. When the workflow is fragmented, speed drops and the client experience suffers.

Cashly CRM was created to give teams a central place for file context, lender-fit thinking, ownership, reminders, and progress tracking so the work feels more controlled from day one.

One shared workspace Keep notes, status, tasks, and documents tied to the file.
Clearer execution Make handoffs and follow-up easier across the team.
Stronger service Support borrowers with faster updates and fewer dropped details.

What we are trying to change
