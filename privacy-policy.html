Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact, identify, support, authenticate, bill, or provide services to You. Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  • Email address
  • First name and last name
  • Phone number
  • Business name, organization name, job title, and account role
  • Billing name, billing address, billing email, and tax-related billing information
  • Payment method information and limited payment metadata, such as payment method type, last four digits of a payment card, expiration date, Stripe customer identifiers, subscription identifiers, invoice records, payment status, and transaction metadata
  • Subscription, plan, credit balance, usage, invoice, refund, and payment history
  • Support requests, account configuration information, and communications with Us

Billing and Payment Data

When You subscribe to paid features, add a payment method, purchase usage credits, maintain a prepaid balance, receive invoices, or use billable services through the Service, We may collect and process Billing Data. Billing Data may include Your billing name, billing address, billing email, payment method type, last four digits of a payment card, expiration date, invoice history, payment status, refund history, tax information, subscription status, account balance, credit balance, usage-based billing records, and transaction metadata.

We use Stripe and its affiliated payment services as Our payment processor. Payment card information is submitted directly to Stripe and is processed by Stripe in accordance with its own privacy and security practices. We do not store full credit card numbers, CVV codes, or complete payment card details on Our own servers.

We may store Stripe customer identifiers, subscription identifiers, payment method references, invoice records, payment status, account balance records, usage records, and related transaction metadata so that We can manage subscriptions, process recurring charges, calculate usage-based charges, reconcile payments, prevent fraud, provide customer support, and maintain accounting, tax, and legal records.

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service.

Usage Data may include information such as Your Device's Internet Protocol address, browser type, browser version, the pages of Our Service that You visit, the time and date of Your visit, the time spent on those pages, unique device identifiers, diagnostic data, application events, login history, feature usage, system activity, and other technical or operational data.

When You access the Service by or through a mobile device, We may collect certain information automatically, including, but not limited to, the type of mobile device You use, Your mobile device's unique ID, the IP address of Your mobile device, Your mobile operating system, the type of mobile Internet browser You use, unique device identifiers, and other diagnostic data.

We may also collect information that Your browser sends whenever You visit Our Service or when You access the Service by or through a mobile device.

Usage-Based Service Data

Certain features of the Service may involve Usage-Based Services. These may include, but are not limited to, telephony, SMS, messaging, call recording, transcription, artificial intelligence usage, AI credits, automation credits, Twilio-related usage, communication services, and other third-party or metered services.

To provide, monitor, support, bill, and rebill Usage-Based Services, We may collect and process related usage records, including call logs, message counts, duration, timestamps, phone numbers involved in communications, recording or transcription metadata, AI usage amounts, token or credit consumption, workflow automation usage, provider usage reports, error logs, billing events, account balances, and invoice line items.

Depending on the features You use, Usage-Based Service Data may be received from third-party providers, including telephony, messaging, AI, automation, infrastructure, or communications providers. We use this information to operate the Service, calculate charges, generate invoices, detect misuse, troubleshoot issues, and provide support.

  • For legal, tax, and compliance purposes: to comply with applicable laws, accounting obligations, tax requirements, audit requirements, regulatory obligations, lawful requests, court orders, and other legal processes.
  • For other purposes: We may use Your information for other purposes, such as data analysis, identifying usage trends, determining the effectiveness of Our promotional campaigns, training and improving Our services, and evaluating and improving Our Service, products, services, marketing, and Your experience.

Retention of Your Personal Data

The Company will retain Your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use Your Personal Data to the extent necessary to comply with Our legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce Our legal agreements and policies, provide the Service, protect against fraud or misuse, and maintain accounting, tax, and billing records.

Where possible, We apply shorter retention periods and/or reduce identifiability by deleting, aggregating, or anonymizing data. Unless otherwise stated, the retention periods below are maximum periods, meaning "up to" the stated period, and We may delete or anonymize data sooner when it is no longer needed for the relevant purpose. We apply different retention periods to different categories of Personal Data based on the purpose of processing and legal obligations:

  • Account Information
    • User Accounts: retained for the duration of Your account relationship plus up to 24 months after account closure to handle any post-termination issues, account recovery, security inquiries, billing questions, or disputes.
  • Billing and Payment Records
    • Invoices, payment history, transaction metadata, subscription records, tax records, refund records, credit records, and usage-based billing records may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the Service, resolve billing disputes, prevent fraud, comply with accounting and tax obligations, and enforce Our agreements.
    • Payment method references, Stripe customer identifiers, subscription identifiers, invoice records, and payment status may be retained for the duration of Your account relationship and for a reasonable period afterward, unless deletion is required or permitted under applicable law and the information is no longer needed for billing, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, tax, or legal compliance.
  • Usage-Based Service Records
    • Usage records for telephony, SMS, calling, call recording, transcription, AI credits, automation credits, Twilio-related usage, communications usage, and other metered services may be retained for as long as needed to calculate charges, generate invoices, reconcile provider charges, provide support, troubleshoot issues, detect misuse, resolve disputes, and comply with legal or accounting obligations.
  • Customer Support Data
    • Support tickets and correspondence: up to 24 months from the date of ticket closure to resolve follow-up inquiries, track service quality, and defend against potential legal claims.
    • Chat transcripts: up to 24 months for quality assurance, staff training, support, and dispute resolution purposes.
  • Usage Data
    • Application usage statistics: up to 24 months to understand feature adoption, service performance, user experience, and service improvements.
    • Server logs, including IP addresses and access times: up to 24 months for security monitoring, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, and incident investigation purposes.

Usage Data is retained in accordance with the retention periods described above, and may be retained longer only where necessary for security, fraud prevention, billing, dispute resolution, tax, accounting, or legal compliance.

We may retain Personal Data beyond the periods stated above for different reasons:

  • Legal obligation: We are required by law to retain specific data, for example financial records for tax authorities.
  • Legal claims: Data is necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • Billing and dispute resolution: Data is necessary to reconcile charges, resolve invoice issues, process refunds, investigate chargebacks, or support payment disputes.
  • Your explicit request: You ask Us to retain specific information.
  • Technical limitations: Data exists in backup systems that are scheduled for routine deletion.

You may request information about how long We will retain Your Personal Data by contacting Us.

When retention periods expire, We securely delete or anonymize Personal Data according to the following procedures:

  • Deletion: Personal Data is removed from Our systems and no longer actively processed.
  • Backup retention: Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period consistent with Our backup retention schedule and are not restored except where necessary for security, disaster recovery, billing, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.
  • Anonymization: In some cases, We convert Personal Data into anonymous statistical data that cannot be linked back to You. This anonymized data may be retained indefinitely for research, analytics, product improvement, and business reporting.
